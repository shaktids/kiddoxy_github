Privacy & Safety

Kiddoxy is designed for children and families. We prioritize privacy and collect minimal data required to run the site.

Data we collect

  • Non-identifying analytics (page views, session length)
  • In-app settings stored locally (no personal names or emails)

Ads

We may show non-personalized ads. We do not target children with behavioral or interest-based ads. Ads are loaded via third-party ad networks — their policies apply. We will document any ad SDKs used here and in the Play Console when publishing an Android app.

Parental controls

Parental gate features are provided to control purchases and certain settings. If you believe a child shared personal information in error, contact us at privacy@example.com
