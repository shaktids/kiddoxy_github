Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions and get help using Kiddoxy.
How do I start a game?
Just tap any game tile on the home page. The game will load instantly in your browser—no downloads or sign-up needed.
Is Kiddoxy safe for children?
Yes! Kiddoxy is designed for young children. We do not collect personal data, and all games are ad-filtered and age-appropriate.
Do I need to install anything?
No installation is required. Kiddoxy works directly in your web browser on any device.
How do I access parental controls?
Click the Parent button in the top right. Enter your birth year to access parental settings and controls.
Who can I contact for support?
Email us at info@kiddoxy.com and we’ll be happy to help!
